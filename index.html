The gesture

One press. Every ball it reaches, shoved.

Beach balls float on open water, each showing a power of two. Everything you do to them, you do through the surface they are sitting on.

  1. 1

    Press and hold

    A ripple charges under your finger. The longer you hold it, the further it will reach and the harder it will shove.

  2. 2

    Read the ring

    A thin ring shows exactly how far the wave will carry, and every ball it would catch lights up — brighter the harder it is about to be hit. That is what makes the shot a decision rather than a guess.

  3. 3

    Let go

    The wavefront travels out at a fixed speed, pushing each ball as it arrives and falling off with distance. Two balls of the same number that collide merge into one of double the value and keep their momentum — so a single well-placed press can set off a chain.

A flick picks off a neighbouring pair. A full hold sweeps about half the board. Either one costs you exactly one move.

The sea gets busier slowly, and it slows down as it goes: a new piece every three boards through the opening coast, every seven by the far end of the chart. Meet it, practise it, then be asked a question with it.

The water

Mines, whirlpools, kelp, crocodiles, and a beaver with a plan.

Every stretch of the voyage introduces one thing and then argues with you about it.

Privacy Policy

Effective: 9 September 2026 Applies to: Ripple: Beach Ball Puzzle for iOS Publisher: Jason Limberis
Ripple collects nothing.

There is no account to make, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party code of any kind in the app. Ripple contains no networking code of its own — it cannot send your information anywhere, because it has no way to send anything anywhere.

01 Information we collect

None. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information about you. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, or location, and the app has no field in which you could give them to us.

We have no server, no database, and no analytics provider. Nothing about your use of Ripple reaches us.

02 Information stored on your device

Ripple saves your progress locally on your iPhone, using the standard storage iOS provides to apps. This information never leaves your device and is not visible to us. It consists of:

  • which islands you have cleared, and how many stars each earned;
  • which detours you have finished and which friends you have found;
  • your power-up inventory and which tutorial cards you have seen;
  • your sound and settings preferences;
  • a record of which purchases you have made, so the app knows what to unlock.

03 Purchases

Ripple offers optional in-app purchases. These are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit, Apple’s own purchasing system. Apple processes the transaction and tells the app only whether a given item has been bought.

We never see or receive your payment card details, billing address, Apple Account credentials, or any other personal information connected to the purchase. Apple’s handling of that transaction is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy, not by this one.

Confirming a purchase, and restoring purchases you have already made, is the only occasion on which the app communicates over the network, and it communicates only with Apple.

04 No tracking, no advertising, no analytics

Ripple contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting service, and no third-party software development kits. It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), does not track you across other apps or websites, and will never show you the App Tracking Transparency prompt — because there is nothing to ask you about.

05 Permissions we do not request

Ripple asks for no system permissions at all. It does not request or access your location, contacts, calendar, photos, camera, microphone, health data, or files.

06 Children

Ripple is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, children included. Because nothing is collected from any user, there is no children’s data for us to hold, disclose, or delete. Parents who wish to prevent in-app purchases can do so using Screen Time on the device.

07 Deleting your information

Because we hold nothing, there is nothing for us to delete on request. Everything Ripple has saved lives on your device: deleting the app removes all of it permanently. You can also clear your progress at any time from Settings → Reset Progress inside the game.

08 App Store privacy label

Ripple’s App Store listing declares the following, which matches this policy exactly:

Data used to track youNone
Data linked to youNone
Data not linked to youNone

09 Changes to this policy

If a future version of Ripple ever changes what it collects, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change with it. Since the app collects nothing today, any such change would be a substantial one, and it would be described plainly here rather than buried.
